## 9. Support reply templates
1. Report not received: "Sorry about that. I can see your order [ID]. I'm checking it now and will send your report or refund you within [X] hours."
2. Wrong birth details: "Thanks for telling us. Please send the correct date, time and place. We'll regenerate your report once at no charge."
3. Refund request, delivered report: "I understand. Per our refund policy, delivered reports aren't refundable unless there was a delivery or data error. If something was wrong with your report, tell me what and I'll look into it."
4. Deletion request: "We'll delete your birth details and reports. Please confirm the phone number you used. This takes up to [Z] days."
5. Upset or distressed message: reply kindly, offer the Tele-MANAS number from section 3, and do not try to sell anything.
