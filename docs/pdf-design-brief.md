# PDF report design brief (draft)

Goal: a short, clear, trustworthy report people will keep and may share. 8-10 pages, A4, English or Hindi.

## Structure
1. Cover: product name, "Career Timing Report", the person's first name (or "for you" if no name), date generated, language, birth-time confidence label (high, medium, lower).
2. Your chart in brief: lagna, Moon sign, nakshatra, one-line plain-language reading of each.
3. The period you are in now: current Mahadasha and Antardasha, in plain language, with dates. (Type C reports show transit-based context instead and say why.)
4. Twelve months at a glance: a simple 12-cell strip, each month marked favourable, neutral or challenging, with colour plus a text label (never colour alone).
5. Your best 2-3 windows: dates, what each window suits (interview, negotiation, switching, starting something), and why.
6. Practical steps: two or three per window. Plain, low-cost.
7. The reasoning (show the math): a table of dasha and transit facts behind each month rating. Generated by the system, not written by the LLM.
8. Disclaimer and about page: the disclaimer text from the content pack; how the report was prepared; how to ask for a correction.

## Style
- Calm, clear, no mystical clutter. Plenty of white space.
- A Devanagari-capable font family for Hindi (for example Noto Sans Devanagari) and a matching Latin font for English. Test conjunct characters and long words on real pages.
- One accent colour. Check contrast. Keep the month strip readable in black and white print.
- Page numbers and the product name in the footer.
- Words to avoid: "doomed", "cursed", "bad luck", "guaranteed", "you will definitely".

## Acceptance
- Hindi and English versions read naturally and are laid out cleanly.
- No text overflows or clipped characters.
- Every date and planet in the text appears in the reasoning table.
- A native reader and an astrologer have reviewed the first 20 reports.
